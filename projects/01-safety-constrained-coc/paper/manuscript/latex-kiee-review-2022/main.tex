\begin{kieeabstract}
Driving VLMs use Chain-of-Causation (CoC) to link scene interpretation and trajectory prediction, but an action objective may omit execution limits. We propose Safety-Constrained CoC, which adds an execution guard, and a paired-contract benchmark that changes only the guard while keeping the scene, objective, and candidates fixed. With LoRA-tuned Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct, standard three-seed temporal tests reduced guard violations from 31.9 percent without guard information to zero, with perfect guard-compliant completion and paired-contract accuracy. On unseen temporal values, candidate accuracy fell to 63.5 percent, violations rose to 35.1 percent, and paired-contract accuracy fell to 30.6 percent. Blank or conflicting images reduced standard temporal accuracy, while maneuver accuracy remained perfect. The results support guard-conditioned synthetic candidate selection, not general numerical extrapolation, continuous trajectory generation, real-road grounding, or vehicle safety.
\end{kieeabstract}
\kieekeywords{Autonomous driving, Vision-language model, Chain-of-Causation, Execution guard, Trajectory selection, Paired-contract benchmark}
