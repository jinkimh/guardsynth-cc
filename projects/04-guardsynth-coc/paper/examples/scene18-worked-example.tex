\subsection{Worked illustration: from a driving reason to conditional supervision}
\label{sec:scene18-worked-example}

A development clip provides the original CoC, ``Yield to the traffic on the main
road and keep a safe distance from the pedestrian crossing the road.''
At the associated event time (9.788430\,s), the CASCADE annotation links an ego
yield action to a walking pedestrian candidate, \texttt{Agent3}. Existing human
review describes a visible hazard over an adjacent time window; it does not
establish an event-time collision predicate. Article~21 of the 1968 Convention
on Road Traffic supplies a pedestrian-protection reference, not an automatic
scene-level legal determination.\footnote{UNECE, Convention on Road Traffic,
Article~21: \url{https://unece.org/DAM/trans/conventn/Conv_road_traffic_EN.pdf}.}
Main-road priority remains a separately conditioned obligation, not a conclusion
derived from that article.

Let $p_t,r_t\in\{T,F,U,C\}$ denote pedestrian conflict and a main-road yielding
obligation, with symbolic evidence-validity inputs $v^p_t,v^r_t$. Define
$o_t=v^p_t\land(p_t=T)$ and $c_t=v^p_t\land(p_t=F)$. For activation $a_t$
and zone-entry action $e_t$, the illustrative policy is
\begin{align}
 a_t &= \begin{cases}
 T & o_t,\\
 F & c_t,\\
 a_{t-1} & \text{otherwise},
 \end{cases} \\
 a_t &\Rightarrow \neg e_t, \qquad
 (v^r_t\land r_t=T)\Rightarrow\neg e_t.
\end{align}
The prior state $a_{-1}$ is a free input. These lifecycle and action choices are
research design assumptions, not measured scene facts or verbatim treaty rules.

Z3~4.16.0 checked the manual encoding over three abstract decisions. All eleven
query premises were satisfiable. Confirmed-conflict entry, unsupported release
of a live obligation, and entry against a confirmed independent yield obligation
were UNSAT; an all-clear entry scenario was SAT. Importantly, entry remained SAT
with an initially inactive pedestrian obligation and UNKNOWN evidence, and with
an UNKNOWN main-road obligation. A review flag alone did not prohibit entry.
Removing the entry prohibition also exposed a violating SAT model. The example
therefore reveals policy gaps rather than reporting a complete safety proof.

The corresponding CNL includes ``While the pedestrian obligation is active,
do not enter the conflict zone,'' together with explicit evidence, retention,
release, reactivation, review and independent-obligation clauses. It must preserve
the policy gaps instead of silently repairing them during rendering. The intended
supervision layout is
\begin{gather*}
 \text{Model input: past/present visual input + common question/actions}\\
 \longmapsto\quad\text{Assistant target: CoC + CNL + independent action label}.
\end{gather*}
No gold CNL is supplied at evaluation. This worked illustration is not automatic
constraint extraction, production EBLC compilation, independently validated CNL,
or real-scene training/effect evidence. The unresolved policy, binding and action
gold prevent its export as a validated training example.
