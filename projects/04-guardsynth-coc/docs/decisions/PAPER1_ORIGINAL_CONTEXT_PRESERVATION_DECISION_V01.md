## Three distinct evidence roles

1. Original CoC is source material and training supervision. Retain its bytes/hash and original
   task, but do not certify its claims as observed truth, normative authority or action gold.
2. Common task context identifies the *same* maneuver/environment being evaluated. Extract
   exact source spans first; a reviewed neutral description may later be shared identically
   with all arms and action evaluators. Preserve task-relevant context instead of hiding it all.
3. Independent action labels must not be copied from the original recommendation, hazard
   polygon, observed driver response, generated CNL or solver. Do not give reviewers the full
   CoC recommendation/reason and call their agreement independent action gold.

A source-derived task context is not proof of a pre-decision navigation log or the driver's
internal intent. The earlier failure to find route logs does not imply every scene lacks usable
task context. Eligibility depends on the selected endpoint, visual interpretability, temporal
policy and absence of answer cues. Retrospective action annotations remain corroboration leads,
not automatically permissible predictive inputs. Conflicts/missing context remain unresolved.

## Endpoint and leakage audit before a replacement survey

Audit all 32 CoCs; retain all in the denominator. Keep exact spans/offsets and source identities.
Separate route/path clues, lateral-action-only cues, recommended behavior and its justification.
Machine lexical extraction is not independent semantic/visual verification or a generic extractor.

Do not turn "steer right" into a right turn, "turning and keeping left" into a left turn, or
"pedestrian on the right" into ego direction. A lane-change phrase can define context for a
longitudinal endpoint but reveals the answer to a lane-change-selection endpoint; review the
pair of context and endpoint, not the text alone. "Cleared", "yield", "resume speed", and
"maintain a safe distance" are not neutral proceed/hold task hints.

The old ENTER_ZONE/DEFER_ENTRY questionnaire is not automatically adequate for deceleration,
speed adaptation, lateral steering, lane change or speed recovery. Determine an original-context
compatible limited action-family/candidate set before freezing gold. Do not add unrelated action
families to the first paper or manufacture a new route to make binary questions fit.

CoCs recommending speed recovery are possible *screening* leads, not nominal gold. Likewise
the eight source FALSE reports are not a progress label. Audit other controls, context and the
independent endpoint; keep abstentions and evaluate violations AND normal-progress preservation.
An always-hold diagnostic remains required; identical-task normal success cannot be replaced by
artificial class balance. Preserve current group/exposure exclusions and the fresh-test gate.
