## Decision

Separate three decisions: all relevant source facts resolved, conditional development example
admissible, and main-study training/evaluation admissible. The first is not a necessary condition
for the second. Neither the first nor the second closes the third. Historical full-source flags,
uploaded answers and completed run artifacts remain unchanged.

A conditional example can retain UNKNOWN when its target/zone and one observed decision are
source-bound and human confirmed; at least one relevant predicate has valid TRUE/FALSE evidence;
remaining UNKNOWN/CONFLICT/invalid evidence stays explicit. The declared CLEAR_REQUIRED_FOR_ENTRY
policy remains unchanged. Missing evidence must not be turned into FALSE, an invented actor,
unobserved release time or numerical geometry. No confirmed input at all remains unresolved.

Required conditional-development gates: verified input identity and causal frame, confirmed target
and nondegenerate zone, explicit relevant-condition mapping, supported contract scope, satisfiable
premises and passing bounded queries, exact document/statement linkage, recorded Korean/English
meaning review and a separately obtained independent development action. An action/specification
disagreement remains an adjudication case in the denominator; do not manufacture agreement from
the solver or silently drop failures. Blinding and expertise are not authenticated by declarations.

Original CoC remains identical in every arm. A directive such as “yield to main-road traffic” is
not itself an observation that a vehicle is present, and is not automatically contradictory to
UNKNOWN. CNL distinguishes source-attributed observations, policy-derived current action and
hypothetical future clearance. A genuine factual or action contradiction requires adjudication;
no automatic rewriting, selective CoC deletion or different action labels between arms.

The implemented entry/defer subset covers reviewed pedestrian/road-yield conditions. Worker signs,
traffic controls and other concurrent obligations are not silently covered by that subset.
Their applicability/scope remains unresolved until reviewed and represented. A pedestrian release
alone never licenses entry past another unresolved applicable control. These rows are retained,
not excluded merely to improve the method's results.

## Main-study requirements remain

Only the blanket “all source predicates resolved” prerequisite is replaced by the explicit
conditional/full-source distinction. Main data still require independent gold/adjudication,
applicable scope coverage, real L0–L3 providers, fixed input sampling, split/exposure checks,
sample-size justification and matched training budgets. A single exposed development record is
not main training admission or held-out evidence. The English conversation response for scene18
is an attributed overall meaning opinion using a prior involvement declaration, not an invented
new per-clause questionnaire or general renderer proof.

Preserve and separately report confirmed-hazard/defer, uncertainty/defer, and confirmed-clear/
nominal-entry cases. Do not call absence of visible hazard a nominal action label. No confirmable
normal-entry examples means normal-progress preservation cannot yet be evaluated. Acquire independent
nominal labels instead of constructing them from a solver. Keep all 32 selected development cases
and their 29 video groups out of the fresh held-out test cohort; cross-clip episode linkage still
needs auditing. No main fine-tuning or effect claim is authorized by admission alone.

## Execution and checks

declared-interval machinery to the 13 unreviewed rows with observations explicitly absent.
Store machine witnesses separately from human predicates/actions. Record conditional admission
reasons, full-source status, human-work gaps, control-scope cases and nominal coverage separately.
Mutations must reject missing geometry, evidence, gold, mapping, review or failed solver checks.
